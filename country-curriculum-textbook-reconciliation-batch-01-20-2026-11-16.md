## Audit rules

- A ministry/authority portal is not evidence that a detailed grade-level topic sequence has been transcribed.
- A curriculum framework or syllabus is not a textbook contents page.
- A textbook catalogue confirms that a title/edition exists; it does not confirm its chapter order.
- No textbook chapter count is assigned unless the contents page of the relevant edition has been inspected.
- Where national curriculum is devolved, community-based, regional or integrated, the notes must preserve that structure rather than force four separate subjects.
- The existing first-10-country sequence audit remains a separate artifact. It covers Kenya, Ghana, Nigeria, Uganda, Tanzania, Albania, Algeria, Andorra, Angola and Antigua and Barbuda; it is not identical to canonical alphabetical countries 1–10.

## Immediate content implications for CBE Nexus

- Andorra Second Education should offer an integrated **Physical and Natural Sciences** pathway, with Mathematics separately; do not fabricate separate Biology/Chemistry/Physics grade chapter lists for that stage.
- Australia must ask or encode the state/territory pathway for senior-secondary course sequence and assessment.
- Austria must respect school type and grade placement; Chemistry is not listed in every class in the cited AHS table.
- Azerbaijan must not show Biology or Chemistry as national standalone subjects in Grades I–VI based on the cited ministry order; Nature and Life Skills are the earlier subject areas.
- Belize lower-secondary notes should retain Integrated Science. Upper-secondary options should be aligned to the actual school pathway and CXC subject selection.
- Bangladesh must separate general secondary from Dakhil and higher-secondary streams, and use the edition-year textbook rather than mixing textbook years.
- Barbados must distinguish CSEC Physics/Mathematics from Integrated Science and Human and Social Biology; these are not interchangeable with four generic national subject tracks.
- Bhutan's textbook availability varies by class and publication rights; do not assume all higher-class texts can be inspected online.

## Remaining work before marking a country complete

For every country in this batch, retrieve the current official syllabus/programme for each applicable grade and stream, transcribe the sequence in source order with page/section references, then open the adopted textbook edition and record its exact title, publisher, edition/year, contents-page image/page, chapter titles and printed chapter numbering. Only after that should CBE Nexus map its authored chapters and add chapter-level SEO/FAQ/schema. This batch is a source and pathway reconciliation; it does not claim 20 textbook audits complete.
