Questions, answered

Before you get started

Do learners need to subscribe to browse teachers?

No. Learners should be able to browse approved public teacher profiles without paying a learner membership fee. Lesson costs, if applicable, are shown separately before booking.

Can teachers choose their hourly rates and currency?

The marketplace is designed for teachers to choose a base hourly rate and currency. Converted prices should be indicative until the actual payment currency and exchange rate are confirmed at checkout.

Does paying for visibility guarantee a higher rank or students?

No. Paid placements should be labelled as promoted. Search relevance, profile quality, verification and learner needs remain important, and no number of bookings or income should be guaranteed.

Can a teacher teach every curriculum worldwide?

Teachers should list only the subjects, levels and curricula they can genuinely support. Licensing, qualification recognition and safeguarding requirements may differ by country and role.

Can I create a teacher account on this page now?

The page currently collects an early-access enquiry through your email app. Secure self-service accounts, saved profiles and payment activation must be connected to the site's backend before full registration can go live.
