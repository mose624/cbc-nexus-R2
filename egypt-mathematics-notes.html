Mathematics study support

Find supplementary mathematics learning support for learners in Egypt. The national school system includes primary, preparatory and secondary stages, and mathematics remains a core subject with additional requirements that can vary by secondary pathway.

Egypt’s secondary pathways are undergoing reform, including the phased introduction of the Egyptian Baccalaureate from the 2026/27 school year. Learners should therefore use the Ministry’s current grade, cohort and pathway guidance rather than assume that one subject combination or textbook sequence applies to every year group.

CBE Nexus provides access to country mathematics notes and practice through the online reader. These resources are supplementary study support; confirm topic order, textbook editions and examination requirements with the Ministry and your school. The site does not claim that its supplementary topic list is the official Egyptian textbook chapter sequence.
