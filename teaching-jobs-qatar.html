Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Qatar teaching vacancies?

Browse the CBE Nexus international vacancies board and follow the official application link provided by each recruiting school.

Do these jobs guarantee visa sponsorship?

No. Sponsorship depends on the employer, role and current immigration rules. Confirm the arrangement directly with the school before accepting an offer.

What should I include in my teacher CV?

Include accurate qualifications, teaching experience, subjects and grade levels, curriculum experience, measurable achievements, professional development and referees. Tailor it to each vacancy.

Can I apply from outside the country?

Some employers consider overseas applicants and others require local eligibility. Check the vacancy's applicant location, work authorisation and relocation conditions.
