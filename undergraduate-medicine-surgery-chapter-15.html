Chapter overview and learning outcomes

This chapter develops an integrated understanding of paediatrics and child health. Learners should explain concepts accurately, connect mechanisms to other disciplines, interpret evidence critically and recognise the limits of simplified models. Patient-related activities require consent, qualified supervision and adherence to institutional and local standards.

  • Define principal terms and classifications.
  • Explain important mechanisms and relationships.
  • Integrate biomedical, clinical and public-health perspectives.
  • Analyse educational cases while acknowledging uncertainty.
  • Apply professional, ethical and patient-safety principles.
