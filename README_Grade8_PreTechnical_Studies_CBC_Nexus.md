# Grade 8 Pre-Technical Studies added to CBC Nexus

This update integrates the supplied Grade 8 Pre-Technical Studies practice examination into `index.html`.

- 30 multiple-choice items (30 marks).
- Topics include materials and tools, workshop safety, technical drawing, structures and mechanisms, electricity and energy, information technology, entrepreneurship and sustainable design.
- Includes suggested answer feedback and explanations. Because the supplied paper did not state answer letters, the answer key was derived from the question content and should be reviewed by a teacher before formal use.
- CBC Nexus applies its global timer rule of two minutes per question (60 minutes).
- This is original practice material, not an official KNEC examination. Confirm alignment against the current KICD curriculum design before formal assessment.
