Global University Courses / Supply Chain Management

About the programme

Procurement, logistics, operations, inventory, transport, supply chain analytics, risk and sustainable sourcing. The discipline combines foundational theory, analytical methods, applied projects, communication and professional responsibility. Students build knowledge progressively and learn to evaluate evidence, solve problems and apply concepts in relevant organisational, social or technical settings.

This illustrative course guide is organised into 16 chapters across four academic years. Course titles, credit weighting, sequence, accreditation, placements and professional requirements vary by institution and country.

Important: This is an educational study framework, not an official university syllabus or professional licence. Confirm current curriculum, accreditation and local legal or professional requirements with the relevant institution and regulator.

Expected learning outcomes

  • Explain foundational and advanced concepts in Supply Chain Management.
  • Apply appropriate analytical, research and problem-solving methods.
  • Interpret evidence, data and discipline-specific information critically.
  • Communicate findings clearly in written, oral and digital formats.
  • Work ethically and collaboratively in relevant professional settings.
  • Complete an applied project demonstrating integrated learning.
