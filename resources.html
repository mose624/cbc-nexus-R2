Quick answer: where can I find CBC and CBE learning resources in Kenya?

CBE Nexus organises learning resources by grade, subject and material type. Start with the learner's grade, confirm the curriculum and topic, then open the relevant notes, practice questions or teacher-planning materials. Availability and prices are shown on individual listings.

For official curriculum requirements, verify the current design with KICD and assessment guidance with KNEC. CBE Nexus is an independent resource directory, not a government examination or curriculum authority.

In short: choose the grade → match the subject and topic → check the resource details → practise and review feedback.

Which CBC and CBE study resources should you use? Benefits and limitations

The best resource depends on the task. Use official curriculum documents to confirm what should be taught; use notes and practice activities to support learning; use teacher-planning materials to organise instruction. These resource types serve different purposes and should not be treated as interchangeable.

Resource typeBest use / benefitsLimitations to check
Official KICD curriculum designsPrimary reference for learning areas, strands, outcomes and curriculum expectations.They are not always a learner-friendly lesson summary or a complete revision pack; check the applicable grade and version.
CBE Nexus notes and study guidesCan make topics easier to review with explanations, examples and organised summaries.Coverage and quality vary by published item. Match each resource to the current curriculum design and check its review details.
Practice questions and assessmentsHelp learners retrieve knowledge, apply ideas and identify gaps before an assessment.A practice score alone does not prove full competency. Check explanations, marking guidance and whether questions are original practice or official past papers.
Schemes of work and lesson plansHelp teachers sequence learning, plan activities and prepare classroom delivery.They need adaptation to the school's timetable, learner needs, available materials and current curriculum guidance.

How to choose a useful learning resource

  1. Confirm that the grade, subject and curriculum match the learner.
  2. Check the topic, term, academic year and assessment type where provided.
  3. Preview available sample pages before ordering a paid item.
  4. For examination preparation, practise questions and review explanations or marking guidance.
  5. Check the listing for price and access conditions; selected materials are free, while others may be paid.

CBE Nexus listings and availability can change as resources are reviewed and published. Always check the individual resource details before relying on a file for lesson planning or examination preparation.

Official curriculum and assessment references

Use these primary sources to verify requirements before relying on a third-party summary or practice resource:

  • Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD): CBC materials — curriculum designs and approved learning materials.
  • KICD curriculum designs directory — official designs by level and grade.
  • KNEC: KPSEA and KJSEA user guide — assessment structure and administration guidance.
  • Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC) — official assessment notices and examination information.

Editorial distinction: KICD and KNEC are the authoritative sources for their respective curriculum and assessment information. CBE Nexus materials are supplementary and should not be presented as official KICD/KNEC publications.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find CBC and CBE resources by grade?

Choose your grade above, then select a subject and the material type you need. You can also open the main Resource Library and use its filters.

What types of learning materials are available?

The library may include notes, schemes of work, lesson plans, records of work, revision questions, assessment tests, past papers, topical questions and marking schemes. Availability varies by grade and subject.

Are all CBE Nexus resources free?

No. Selected materials are free, while some resources may require payment. Each listing shows its access and price details before an order is placed.

Where can Grade 9 learners find KJSEA revision?

Visit the KJSEA revision page and the Grade 9 sections of the resource library and interactive questions. Check each listing for current availability.

Can learners practise questions online?

Yes. Visit Quizzes and Interactive Questions to see the practice activities currently published for each grade and subject.
