IB Diploma Programme learning and assessment support

Plan learning across the programme: confirm the subject, level and current subject guide; map concepts and skills; complete inquiry and application tasks; practise assessment-style responses; and reflect on feedback. The Diploma Programme also includes Theory of Knowledge, the Extended Essay and Creativity, Activity, Service.
Subject knowledgeConcepts, key terms, models, case studies and subject-specific skills.
Inquiry and analysisResearch questions, evidence evaluation, interpretation and critical thinking.
Assessment practiceCommand terms, response structure, data analysis and timed practice.
DP Core guidancePlanning and reflection support for TOK, Extended Essay and CAS.

Coverage note: IB subject content and assessment requirements can change. Check the current IB subject guide and your school’s course requirements.
