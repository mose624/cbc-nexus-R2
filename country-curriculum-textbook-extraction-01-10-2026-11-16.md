- A textbook contents record is recorded only when the actual official/approved book PDF has been opened and its contents page inspected.
- Curriculum framework structure, official book catalogue entries, and textbook metadata are recorded separately from syllabus topic sequences and contents-page transcriptions.
- Where official PDFs are inaccessible or a current edition cannot be confirmed, status remains open. Do not substitute a third-party syllabus or an assumed sequence.

## 9. Australia

https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/curriculum-information/understand-this-learning-area/science/
