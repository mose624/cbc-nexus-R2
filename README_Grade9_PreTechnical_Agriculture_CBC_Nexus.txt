CBC Nexus — Grade 9 assessment update

Included new Grade 9 assessments
1. Pre-Technical Studies
   - 10 multiple-choice questions and 10 structured/drawing/calculation questions.
   - One-attempt learner workflow and two minutes per question (40 minutes total).
   - The source did not specify marks for Question 11. This version uses a provisional 5-mark rubric for the three orthographic views, giving a suggested total of 55 marks (10 MCQ marks + 45 structured marks). A teacher may revise this allocation before formal use.
   - Question 6 was clarified to specify a front-facing orthographic view, since a plain rectangle without context does not uniquely identify front/top/side view.
   - Technical drawing, circuit, triangle, bench vice and marking-out diagrams are illustrative.

2. Agriculture & Nutrition
   - 20 multiple-choice questions and 6 structured questions, 50 marks total.
   - Includes hay making, integrated farming, grain storage, grafting, solar drying, safe waste handling, food conservation, compost and organic gardening.
   - Suggested time from source: 90 minutes. CBC Nexus site-wide timing rule is 2 minutes per question, which gives 52 minutes for 26 question items.

Both papers retain the website's learner-name/school/stream gate, one-attempt workflow, score/progress tracking, and per-question answer/marking data. Automatic written marking across devices requires the Supabase backend and AI secret to be configured and deployed; see README_AI_WRITTEN_MARKING_AND_TIMING.md.

These are practice assessments, not official KNEC examination papers. Check question wording, marks, and curriculum coverage before formal use.
