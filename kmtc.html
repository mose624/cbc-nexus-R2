Kenya Medical Training College (KMTC): applicant guide

The Kenya Medical Training College (KMTC) is a major public provider of health-related training in Kenya, with campuses serving learners in different regions. Health programmes involve academic study and supervised practical or clinical learning. Applicants should verify the current course list, campus availability, entry criteria and professional requirements for the exact intake.

Choose a health profession first

KMTC study pathways span different health disciplines and qualification levels. Understand the work performed by the profession, typical work settings, patient-care responsibilities and additional registration or licensing steps that may apply after graduation.

Check current course and campus availability

A course may not be offered at every campus or in every intake. Consult current official KMTC admissions information and confirm campus, programme title, duration, entry requirements and deadline. Do not rely on old posters or unofficial lists.

Clinical learning and professional conduct

Health training commonly combines classroom learning, laboratory or simulation work and supervised clinical experience. Ask about placements, assessment methods, attendance, uniforms or equipment, infection prevention and professional conduct.

Registration and licensing

Graduation and professional registration are separate steps where a regulator is responsible. Confirm the applicable professional council or board, examination requirements, internship or supervised practice conditions and registration process.

Fees and safe applications

Use official KMTC channels to confirm tuition, accommodation, clinical or practical costs, payment instructions and refund policies. Never pay an intermediary who cannot be verified through the college.

Frequently asked questions

Are all KMTC courses offered at every campus?

No. Course and campus availability can vary by intake.

Does graduation automatically provide a professional licence?

Not necessarily. Verify the relevant regulator’s registration and licensing requirements.

Where should I apply?

Follow current official KMTC admissions instructions and authorised application channels.

Official sources and further checks

Use the Commission for University Education (CUE) for university status and quality-assurance information, and KUCCPS for current placement and programme information where applicable. For TVET and technical qualifications, also consult the relevant TVET regulator and the institution’s official admissions office. Always verify current information before applying or paying.
