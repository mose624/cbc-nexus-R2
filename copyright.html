Copyright & Intellectual Property

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Respect for copyright

CBE Nexus expects users and sellers to respect copyright, trademarks and other intellectual-property rights. Uploading or selling material does not transfer ownership of content to CBE Nexus.

Seller responsibility

Before submitting a resource, sellers must have the rights or permission necessary to distribute it. Sellers must not upload unauthorized copies of textbooks, examination papers, teacher materials, images, videos or other protected works.

Reporting a concern

If you believe content on CBE Nexus infringes your copyright or other rights, contact CBE Nexus with the resource title or URL, a description of the protected work, the reason for your claim and your contact details. We may temporarily restrict or remove disputed content while the matter is reviewed.

Good-faith reporting

Reports should be accurate and made in good faith. False or abusive reports may be disregarded.

Contact

Use the contact details published on the CBE Nexus Contact page for intellectual-property complaints and supporting documents.
