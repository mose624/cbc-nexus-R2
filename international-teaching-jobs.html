Find opportunities that may accept internationally trained teachers. Always verify the state licensing, visa and credential requirements attached to each position.

🌏 Asia

Explore international and bilingual school opportunities across Asian markets. Requirements vary by country, school and curriculum.

🌎 South America

International schools in South America can recruit teachers for English-medium and international curricula. Review language, qualification and work-permit requirements carefully.

🌍 Europe & other regions

Search international schools and education organisations in Europe, Africa, Oceania and other regions where overseas teachers are recruited.

📄 Application preparation

Prepare a professional CV, cover letter, teaching portfolio, references and supporting documents before applying.

Important: A vacancy does not automatically mean a teacher is eligible to work in that country. Confirm current qualification recognition, teacher licensing, visa/work-permit, experience and safeguarding requirements directly with the employer and relevant authorities.
