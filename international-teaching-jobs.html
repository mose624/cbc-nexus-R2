⭐ What makes an international teacher CV unique?

Don't just list duties. Show evidence of teaching ability, impact and international readiness.

🎯 Tailor it to the vacancy

Use the exact role, subject, grade level and curriculum language that genuinely matches your experience.

📈 Show measurable impact

Where truthful, show outcomes: improved results, projects delivered, resources created, competitions, leadership or student progress.

🌍 Show curriculum strength

Clearly identify CBC/CBE, Cambridge, IGCSE, IB, Pearson Edexcel, British, American or other curriculum experience you actually have.

🧑‍🏫 Show classroom expertise

Highlight lesson planning, assessment, differentiation, classroom management, practical work, safeguarding and student-centred teaching.

💻 Demonstrate digital teaching

Include relevant LMS, online teaching, presentation, assessment and educational technology skills.

🤝 Demonstrate multicultural readiness

Show collaboration, communication, adaptability and experience working with diverse learners, colleagues and communities.

🚀 How to become a stronger candidate

Use your CV to tell an evidence-based professional story.

1. Build a master CV

Keep a complete version containing all relevant experience, achievements, qualifications and professional development.

2. Tailor every application

Adapt the profile, skills and achievement bullets to the exact role instead of sending the same CV everywhere.

3. Make evidence visible

Replace generic claims with truthful examples of what you did, how you did it and what changed because of your work.

4. Research the school

Understand its curriculum, student population, teaching philosophy and expectations before writing your application.

5. Prepare supporting documents

Keep certificates, transcripts, registration documents, references, passport and other requested documents organised.

6. Verify eligibility

Check qualification recognition, teacher licensing, visa/work permit, experience, safeguarding and language requirements.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most important part of an international teacher CV?

The combination of a strong professional profile, relevant teaching experience, qualifications/licensing, curriculum knowledge, measurable achievements and skills that match the vacancy. The strongest CV is tailored rather than generic.

Should I include every job I have ever had?

Prioritise relevant teaching and professional experience. Older or unrelated roles can be shortened when they do not strengthen the application.

Should I put a photo on my CV?

Photo expectations differ by country, employer and vacancy. Do not assume a photo is required. Follow the destination and employer's stated requirements.

How long should my CV be?

Use the shortest length that communicates your relevant evidence well. Early-career applicants may often need less space, while experienced teachers may need more. Avoid padding the CV just to reach a page count.

Can I claim a curriculum I have never taught?

No. The builder is designed to present genuine experience. If you have related skills or training, describe that accurately rather than claiming experience you do not have.

Does CBE Nexus guarantee a job or visa?

No. CBE Nexus can help with preparation and discovery, but the employer and relevant authorities determine hiring, qualification recognition, licensing and immigration eligibility.

Important: International vacancies and immigration requirements change. Always verify the current vacancy, employer, qualification-recognition rules, teacher licensing, safeguarding requirements and visa/work-permit conditions with the employer and relevant official authorities before paying fees or travelling.
