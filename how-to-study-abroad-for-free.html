CBE NEXUS EDUCATION GUIDE · SCHOLARSHIPS

How Can I Study Abroad for Free?

Look for fully funded government scholarships, university awards, exchange programmes and public universities with low or no tuition. Examples to research include DAAD, Chevening, Fulbright and Erasmus+. Eligibility, covered costs and deadlines differ by programme and year, so use the official provider's website.

Plan for the full cost

Calculate accommodation, food, flights, insurance, visa fees and books as well as tuition. Prepare transcripts, references, a CV and a personal statement early. Some programmes require admission before scholarship consideration. Apply through official channels and be cautious of anyone promising a guaranteed award for a fee.

International standards: expanded guidance

Look for funding before choosing a destination

Search government scholarships, university awards and international programmes that may cover tuition and living costs. Examples include Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters, Chevening, Fulbright and DAAD opportunities. Check nationality, study level, course eligibility, covered expenses and current application windows on each official programme site.

Compare tuition-free and low-tuition options carefully

Some public universities charge low or no general tuition for certain students or programmes, but exceptions and mandatory semester fees may apply. Fee rules can differ for international students, degree levels, states or institutions. For Germany, for example, confirm the exact university and course fee policy rather than assuming all programmes are free.

Budget for living and arrival costs

Calculate rent, food, transport, health insurance, books, visa and residence-permit fees, flights, deposits and initial settlement. Some countries require evidence of funds before issuing a student visa. A scholarship may pay a stipend monthly rather than covering every expense upfront.

Check admission, language and visa requirements

Confirm academic prerequisites, language tests, recognition of prior qualifications and document translation rules. Review student work restrictions and scholarship conditions; do not assume part-time work will pay all expenses. Apply through official university and scholarship portals and keep copies of every offer and visa document.
