How to study effectively with CBE Nexus

1. Confirm your specification

Check the awarding organisation, qualification, subject code, syllabus version and examination session before using a resource.

2. Learn topic by topic

Use clear notes and worked examples to understand concepts. Build a topic checklist from the official syllabus.

3. Practise and review

Attempt questions under timed conditions, mark your work using permitted guidance, and revisit the topics where you lose marks.

4. Track your coverage

Record completed topics, practical work, assignments and revision sessions. Do not assume a directory listing means all content is already published.

Frequently asked questions

Which international curricula are covered on CBE Nexus?

Dedicated sections are available for Cambridge International qualifications, the International Baccalaureate and Pearson Edexcel.

Does a subject appearing on the directory mean its full syllabus is complete?

No. A subject listing is a navigation entry, not a claim that every topic or resource is complete. Check the subject page for published content and coverage notes.

How should I choose the right study resources?

Confirm the awarding organisation, qualification, subject and syllabus or specification code. Then compare the material with the current official syllabus and examination requirements.

Are all examination resources free?

Access conditions vary by item. Review each resource listing for its price, preview and access details before proceeding.
