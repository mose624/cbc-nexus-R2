# CBC Nexus — Grade 7 Integrated Science addition

- Added a Grade 7 Integrated Science bank of 30 HOTS multiple-choice questions.
- Each question has four options, a suggested correct option, and a brief explanation.
- Topics include fair testing and laboratory safety, ecosystems, matter, materials, forces, energy, health, Earth and space, sustainability, and integrated problem-solving.
- The subject is available in the Grade 7 assessment hub and subject bank.
- This is an original practice assessment, not an official KNEC examination.
- The paper specifies 45 minutes; CBC Nexus retains its existing two-minute-per-question timer, which makes 60 minutes for 30 questions.
- The answer key was derived from the supplied questions because no separate answer key was included.
