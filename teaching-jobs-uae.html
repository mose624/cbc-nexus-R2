Where to look for UAE teaching jobs

International schools and education groups recruit teachers across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and other emirates. Search by subject, grade level and curriculum, then confirm the campus location and employer details in each vacancy.

What schools may ask applicants for

Requirements vary by employer and role. Be ready to provide recognised teaching qualifications, relevant classroom experience, subject expertise, professional references and any required document attestations or local approvals.

Visa and relocation checks

Do not assume that every vacancy includes sponsorship. Confirm in writing whether the employer supports the required work authorisation, what costs are covered and whether dependants are included.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find UAE teaching vacancies?

Browse the CBE Nexus international vacancies board and follow the official application link provided by each recruiting school.

Do these jobs guarantee visa sponsorship?

No. Sponsorship depends on the employer, role and current immigration rules. Confirm the arrangement directly with the school before accepting an offer.

What should I include in my teacher CV?

Include accurate qualifications, teaching experience, subjects and grade levels, curriculum experience, measurable achievements, professional development and referees. Tailor it to each vacancy.

Can I apply from outside the country?

Some employers consider overseas applicants and others require local eligibility. Check the vacancy's applicant location, work authorisation and relocation conditions.
