Selected fully worked solutions

Worked solution — Question 6: completing the square

y=x²−6x+5=(x−3)²−9+5=(x−3)²−4. The turning point is (3,−4), the axis is x=3, and the curve opens upwards. For x-intercepts, (x−3)²=4, giving x=1 or x=5. At x=0, y=5.

Worked solution — Question 12: modulus inequality

|x+1|<4 gives −4<x+1<4. Subtracting 1 gives −5<x<3. Both endpoints are excluded because the inequality is strict.

Worked solution — Question 15: equal roots

The discriminant is zero: (k+2)²−8k=0. Expanding gives k²−4k+4=0, hence (k−2)²=0 and k=2. The equation becomes (x−2)²=0, with repeated root x=2.

Worked solution — Question 41: rational inequality

The critical values are x=−1 and x=2. Testing intervals gives positive signs on (−∞,−1) and (2,∞), and a negative sign on (−1,2). Include x=2 because the expression equals zero; exclude x=−1 because it is undefined. Thus x<−1 or x≥2.
