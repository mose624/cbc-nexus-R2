IGCSE topic mastery and exam preparation

Study sequence: confirm the subject and syllabus code, map every syllabus statement, learn each topic, practise structured questions, review mistakes and complete timed papers. For sciences, include practical methods, variables, apparatus, safety and data interpretation.
Understand conceptsDefinitions, explanations, worked examples and key vocabulary.
Apply knowledgeCalculations, unfamiliar contexts, source analysis and structured responses.
Develop practical skillsMethods, variables, observations, graphs, evaluation and safety.
Prepare for assessmentCommand words, mark allocation, timed practice and error correction.

Coverage note: Subject codes and assessment components vary by syllabus and examination session. Confirm the current syllabus with Cambridge International.
