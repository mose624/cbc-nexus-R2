About this learning pathway

Biotechnology applies biological knowledge, organisms, cells, biomolecules and biological processes to develop or improve products, methods and services. It brings together cell biology, molecular biology, biochemistry, genetics, microbiology, bioinformatics, engineering, statistics, ethics and regulation.

This independent study guide is designed for undergraduate learning and revision. It is not an accredited degree, professional licence or substitute for an institution's current curriculum, laboratory manual, supervised practical work or biosafety training. Course sequencing and professional requirements vary between institutions and countries.

How to study

  • Read the topic explanations and create a glossary of scientific terminology.
  • Redraw diagrams and explain the mechanisms without looking at the notes.
  • Practise experimental design, controls, data interpretation and scientific reporting.
  • Use current textbooks, peer-reviewed research and official technical guidance for deeper study.
  • Only undertake practical biological work in an approved and appropriately supervised laboratory.
