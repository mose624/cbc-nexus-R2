Bachelor of Social Work

A four-year undergraduate learning framework for understanding people, families, groups, communities and social institutions. Develop knowledge in ethical social work practice, human behaviour, social policy, advocacy, research and community-led change.

About this Social Work degree study guide

This course guide organises core undergraduate social work learning into 16 chapters. It connects foundational knowledge with practice skills, critical reflection, evidence-informed interventions and the social conditions that shape wellbeing. Examples draw on local, regional and international settings; students should interpret law, professional regulation and service pathways in the jurisdiction where they study or practise.

Educational study framework only. It is not an accredited university programme, a substitute for supervised field education, or legal/clinical advice. Programme requirements vary by institution and country.
