👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Guide

Should we choose a school only because it is highly ranked?

No. Compare pathway fit, available tracks and subjects, learner interests, school environment, accommodation, location and other verified information. The official placement process considers multiple factors.

What pathways should we consider?

The senior school framework includes STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts & Sports. The official subject-combination list should be used when checking the subjects available for a particular pathway and track.

Can CBE Nexus guarantee placement?

No. CBE Nexus is a discovery, comparison and planning tool. Final placement is determined through the official process.

📌 Important Information

The Kenya National Examinations Council says KJSEA is designed to guide learners in choosing senior-school pathways. The official subject-combination document should be checked for current combinations, and the Ministry selection portal should be used for official selection and placement information.

CBE Nexus Match Score is an independent guidance score generated from the information entered on this page. It is not an official KNEC score, Ministry ranking, admission score or placement guarantee.
