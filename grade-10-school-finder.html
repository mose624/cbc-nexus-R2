How to choose a Senior School

1. Pathway: consider the learner's demonstrated competencies, interests and aspirations.

2. School fit: compare cluster, location, gender, accommodation, facilities and available learning areas.

3. Verify: use the official Ministry selection system for current school profiles, subject combinations and placement information.

CBE Nexus comparisons are an independent research tool, not an official Ministry of Education or KNEC ranking.

Grade 10 Selection 2027 FAQs

What pathways are available?

STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts & Sports.

What are C1, C2, C3 and C4?

They are Senior School cluster categories used in the Grade 10 selection process.

Where should I verify school information?
