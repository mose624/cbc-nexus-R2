Quick answer: how do I choose a Grade 10 pathway and senior school in Kenya?

Use the learner's interests, demonstrated strengths, subject preferences and career aspirations to guide pathway exploration. Compare schools by the pathways and subjects they actually offer, location, accommodation and learner support. Then confirm current selection instructions and placement through the official Ministry of Education portal.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Guide

Should we choose a school only because it is highly ranked?

No. Compare pathway fit, available tracks and subjects, learner interests, school environment, accommodation, location and other verified information. The official placement process considers multiple factors.

What pathways should we consider?

The senior school framework includes STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts & Sports. The official subject-combination list should be used when checking the subjects available for a particular pathway and track.

Can CBE Nexus guarantee placement?

No. CBE Nexus is a discovery, comparison and planning tool. Final placement is determined through the official process.

📌 Important Information

The Kenya National Examinations Council says KJSEA is designed to guide learners in choosing senior-school pathways. The official subject-combination document should be checked for current combinations, and the Ministry selection portal should be used for official selection and placement information.

CBE Nexus Match Score is an independent guidance score generated from the information entered on this page. It is not an official KNEC score, Ministry ranking, admission score or placement guarantee.

Grade 10 pathway and school selection: common questions

What does the Grade 10 school finder help a learner do?

It helps learners and parents organise information about learner strengths, interests, pathways, subject combinations and senior-school options before making school choices.

Is this the official Ministry of Education placement portal?

No. CBE Nexus provides independent planning and discovery support. Use the official Ministry of Education selection portal for current instructions and official placement actions.

What should parents compare when shortlisting senior schools?

Compare the pathways and subjects offered, school category, location, accommodation, learner support and any current eligibility or selection requirements. Confirm details with the school and official Ministry sources.
