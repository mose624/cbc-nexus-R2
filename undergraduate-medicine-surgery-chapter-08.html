Chapter overview and learning outcomes

This chapter develops an integrated understanding of pharmacology and therapeutics. Learners should explain concepts accurately, connect mechanisms to other medical disciplines, interpret evidence critically and recognise the limits of simplified models. Patient-related activities require appropriate consent, qualified supervision and adherence to institutional and local standards.

  • Define principal terms and classifications.
  • Explain important structures, mechanisms and relationships.
  • Integrate knowledge across biomedical and clinical disciplines.
  • Analyse educational cases while acknowledging uncertainty.
  • Apply professional, ethical and patient-safety principles.

Pharmacology examines how medicines and chemical agents interact with living systems. It includes pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, therapeutic use, toxicology and medicine safety. Effects depend on dose, formulation, route, patient characteristics, interactions and the clinical question.
