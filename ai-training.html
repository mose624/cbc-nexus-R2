Quick answer: what can I learn in the CBE Nexus AI Academy?

The AI Academy offers self-paced courses in AI foundations, prompt engineering, AI for teaching, productivity, responsible AI and applied digital skills. Choose a course that matches your current level, work through its modules, practise with the activities and check your understanding before applying the skill in real work.

For teachers: use AI as a support tool, not as an unchecked authority. Verify generated facts, protect learner information and keep human judgement central to teaching.

Frequently asked questions about free AI training

Are the CBE Nexus AI Academy courses self-paced?

The Academy describes its courses as self-paced. Open an individual course page to review its modules, learning activities and any course-specific requirements.

Do I need programming experience to start learning AI?

Not necessarily. Begin with AI Foundations if you are new to the subject. Programming becomes useful for more technical topics such as building applications, automation and machine learning.

Can teachers use AI-generated lesson notes directly in class?

They should review and adapt them first. Check facts, curriculum alignment, learner age, cultural context, accessibility and possible bias before classroom use.

Is a certificate guaranteed for every free AI course?

Certificate availability and requirements can differ by course. Check the course page and the Academy certificate information before assuming a certificate is included.

What information should teachers avoid entering into AI tools?

Avoid entering identifiable learner information, private school records, assessment records or confidential personal data unless your institution has approved the tool and the data use is lawful and appropriately protected.
