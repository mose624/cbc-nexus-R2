How to find genuine free AI training

  • Check that course lessons are free and not only the registration page.
  • Find out whether certificates, exams or graded assessments cost extra.
  • Review the provider, course outline, language and expected study time.
  • Confirm that registration and course access are available in Bangladesh.
  • Never submit passwords, payment information or confidential personal data to unverified websites or AI tools.
