About us

About CBE Nexus

CBE Nexus connects learners, teachers, parents, and schools with easy-to-find CBC and CBE learning resources for Kenya. The platform is designed to make teaching, revision, assessment and resource discovery simpler.

Resources include notes, exams, revision questions, schemes of work, lesson plans, records of work, quizzes, assessment support and other teacher and learner materials.

Frequently asked questions

CBE Nexus FAQ

Quick answers about CBC and CBE resources, payments, free materials and support.

What is CBE Nexus?

CBE Nexus is a Kenyan learning-resource platform connecting learners, teachers, parents and schools with CBC and CBE educational materials.

Which resources can I find?

You can find notes, schemes of work, lesson plans, records of work, assessment tests, topical questions, revision materials, marking schemes, quizzes and other learning resources.

How do I find a resource?

Use the Resource Library and filter by grade, subject, term, resource type or keyword. You can also use the quick resource links on the homepage.

Are there free CBC resources?

Yes. Visit the Free CBC Resources section for selected materials available at no cost.

How do I pay for a resource?

Follow the payment instructions shown for your order. CBE Nexus uses M-Pesa payment confirmation and WhatsApp support for order assistance.

Can teachers sell resources?

Approved teachers and sellers can submit suitable CBC/CBE learning materials through the resource submission process, subject to platform review.

How can I get support?

Use the Customer Service assistant or contact CBE Nexus through the official contact and WhatsApp support options on the website.
