1. Free teacher CV writing and international CV support

Create and review a clear, professional teacher CV that presents qualifications, teaching experience, curriculum knowledge, subject expertise, classroom achievements and references. Use role-specific language and check that dates, credentials and contact details are accurate. An ATS-friendly layout can make information easier for recruitment systems and school HR teams to process, but it cannot guarantee selection.

Teachers applying from Bulgaria should tailor each application to the destination school's requirements. Check whether the employer requests a teaching licence, qualification assessment, document attestation, language evidence or specific safeguarding checks.

Explore teaching vacancies by subject, grade, curriculum and destination. Compare the advertised qualifications, experience, contract length, salary and benefits.
