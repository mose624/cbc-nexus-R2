Specialised Universities in Kenya: applicant guide

Some Kenyan higher-education institutions have a focused mandate rather than the broad subject range associated with a comprehensive university. Their missions may centre on open and distance learning, defence and security, science, technology or research. Applicants should assess each institution against its legal mandate and the exact qualification offered.

What makes an institution specialised?

A specialised institution concentrates teaching, research or professional development on a defined field or mode of delivery. Its focus can shape admission criteria, programme design, research partnerships and facilities. The term ‘specialised’ is descriptive; verify formal legal status and authority to award the qualification.

Match the programme to the mandate

Read the programme description and curriculum. Check qualification level, learning outcomes, practical or research components, duration and progression route. Specialist programmes may target school leavers, working professionals or applicants who already hold a relevant qualification.

Check delivery and access

Institutions with distance, online or professional-learning mandates may use different application and teaching processes. Ask about admission windows, platforms, study centres, supervised assessments, residential requirements and specialist facilities.

Recognition and professional pathways

Confirm institutional status with CUE or the relevant statutory authority, and verify programme recognition with the professional regulator when the career is regulated. A degree title alone does not guarantee professional registration or employment eligibility.

Plan for a specialist learning environment

Consider whether the institution’s focus, research culture, location and student support match your goals. Ask about mentorship, partnerships, research supervision and career services.

Frequently asked questions

Does specialised mean only one course is offered?

No. A specialist institution may offer several programmes within its approved mandate.

Are all specialist qualifications professionally recognised?

Recognition depends on the specific programme and profession. Check the relevant regulator.

Where can I verify degree-awarding status?

Use current CUE information and the institution’s official legal and programme details.
