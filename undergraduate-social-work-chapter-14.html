Chapter learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter, learners should be able to explain key concepts, compare relevant frameworks, apply knowledge to realistic practice situations, identify ethical and safeguarding considerations, evaluate intervention options and communicate reasoned decisions. Learners should also recognise the limits of their competence and the need for supervision, local legal guidance and collaboration.

  • Define and critically discuss the chapter's central concepts.
  • Connect individual experiences with family, community, institutional and structural contexts.
  • Apply an organised, participatory and evidence-informed practice process.
  • Identify risks, rights, resources and ethical responsibilities.
  • Evaluate outcomes and propose realistic improvements.
