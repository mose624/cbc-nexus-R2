Open Ghana Mathematics Notes

Mathematics study support

Explore mathematics learning support for Ghanaian learners across Primary Basic 1–6, Junior High School 1–3 and Senior High School 1–3. The NaCCA curriculum distinguishes the primary Standards-Based Curriculum, the JHS Common Core Programme and the SHS subject curriculum; these phases should not be treated as one identical syllabus.

CBE Nexus includes supplementary mathematics learning material such as number systems and sets, algebraic expressions and equations, geometry, angles, bearings, trigonometry and mensuration. Use the notes for explanation and practice, and check the current NaCCA curriculum and your school’s scheme of learning for the official scope and order.

Assessment context: BECE and WASSCE preparation should follow the current examination requirements and subject guidance issued by the relevant authorities. These CBE Nexus resources are supplementary learning support, not official NaCCA textbooks or examination papers.
