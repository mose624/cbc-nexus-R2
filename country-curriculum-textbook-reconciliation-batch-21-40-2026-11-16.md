# CBE Nexus — curriculum and textbook reconciliation, batch 02 (canonical countries 21–40)

Audit date: 2026-11-16  
Repository: `mose624/cbc-nexus-R2`  
Scope: entries 21–40 in `country-curriculum-chapter-completion-register.js`, canonical alphabetical order.

## Audit rules

- A national framework, ministry portal or exam syllabus is not automatically a complete grade-by-grade topic sequence.
- Examination syllabi describe assessment requirements; they are not substitutes for the full curriculum.
- A textbook catalogue or programme guide is not a textbook contents page. Chapter order/counts remain unverified until the relevant edition's contents pages are inspected.
- Where curriculum authority is decentralized, provincial, state, entity, canton, district or local pathways must be recorded separately.
- The register remains a planning and quality-control record. This report does not mark any subject's chapter sequence or textbook contents pages complete.
