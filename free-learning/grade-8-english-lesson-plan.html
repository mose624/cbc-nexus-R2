Grade 8 English Lesson Plan: Reading for Meaning and Inference

Prepared by the CBE Nexus Education Content Team · Updated 14 November 2026 · Read online at no cost

A free 40-minute Grade 8 English lesson plan with an original reading passage, vocabulary, comprehension questions, answers and assessment guidance.

Learning area: English · Topic: Reading comprehension · Duration: 40 minutes · Approach: Guided reading and discussion

Learning outcomes

By the end of the lesson, learners should be able to read a short passage fluently; identify the main idea and supporting details; infer a character's feelings from evidence; explain selected words in context; and answer comprehension questions in complete sentences.

Learning resources

Use the passage below, a board or chart, learner exercise books and a dictionary. Learners work individually, in pairs and as a whole class.

Original reading passage: The school garden

When the dry season began, the learners at Mwangaza Junior School noticed that the young trees near their classrooms were wilting. The environmental club suggested collecting rainwater in covered containers and using it carefully. On Friday, the club invited other learners to clear weeds, loosen the soil and place dry leaves around the seedlings. A week later, the leaves looked greener. Amina recorded the amount of water used each day, while Brian reminded the team to keep the containers covered. The head teacher praised the group, but asked them to continue monitoring the plants rather than assuming the problem was solved. The learners agreed to take turns watering the garden and sharing their observations during assembly.

Comprehension and language questions

  1. Why did the learners become concerned about the trees?
  2. What three actions did the club take on Friday?
  3. Why did Brian remind the team to cover the containers?
  4. What does “wilting” suggest about the condition of the trees?
  5. What can you infer about Amina from her task?
  6. Why did the head teacher ask the group to keep monitoring the plants?
  7. Give a suitable alternative title for the passage.
  8. Write one sentence explaining how teamwork helped the project.
Suggested answers
  1. The trees were wilting as the dry season began.
  2. They cleared weeds, loosened the soil and placed dry leaves around the seedlings.
  3. Covered containers help keep stored water clean and reduce evaporation or contamination.
  4. The trees were losing freshness and becoming weak because they lacked enough water.
  5. She is observant and responsible because she kept a daily record.
  6. The plants still needed care and the improvement had to be checked over time.
  7. For example, “Caring for Our School Garden”.
  8. Answers should explain that learners shared tasks and supported one another.

Assessment and differentiation

Formative assessment: listen to oral reading, check pair discussions and assess written responses for evidence from the passage, clarity and sentence construction.

Support: pre-teach vocabulary, read the passage in short sections and provide the sentence starter “I infer that … because the passage says …”. Extension: ask confident learners to write a 100-word report proposing a practical way to conserve water at school.

Homework

Write one paragraph about a school or community activity that required teamwork. Include a topic sentence, two supporting details and a concluding sentence.
