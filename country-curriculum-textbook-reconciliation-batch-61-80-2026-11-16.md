## Evidence quality and chapter-entry gate

- **Official framework/programme leads found:** France, Germany, Ghana, Greece, Guatemala, Guyana, Honduras.
- **Actual textbook contents page surfaced:** India — NCERT Class XI Biology (2025–26 reprint). Its visible contents list includes Units I–III and Chapters 1–10; this is one textbook, not evidence of all India curricula.
- **No actual textbook contents pages inspected for the other 19 countries in this batch.**
- **No complete official grade-by-grade topic sequence transcribed into the canonical register in this batch.**
- **No chapter counts or chapter completion statuses should be fabricated.**
- Frameworks, curriculum portals, examination syllabi and textbook catalogue entries are source leads only. A textbook's actual contents page must be opened and checked before its chapter titles are entered as textbook-derived data.

## Required next pass

1. Retrieve official subject documents for each source-gap country, prioritising Gabon, The Gambia, Georgia, Grenada, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Haiti, Hungary, Iceland, Indonesia, Iran and Iraq.
2. For France, Germany, Ghana, Greece, Guatemala, Guyana and Honduras, retrieve the exact grade/class/year documents and transcribe topic order without blending tracks or jurisdictions.
3. For India, record NCERT Class XI Biology contents as one explicitly identified textbook edition; then inspect matching Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics contents pages and separately identify board/state alternatives.
4. Add only source-backed grade/topic sequences to the canonical register. Keep official syllabus topics, textbook chapter titles and CBE Nexus supplementary content in separate fields.
5. Do not claim a country's chapter coverage complete until the relevant syllabus sequence and actual textbook contents have both been reconciled.
