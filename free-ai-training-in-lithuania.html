Practical AI projects for learners in Lithuania

Lithuanian- and English-language learning, education, technology, manufacturing, research and business. Use AI as an assistant rather than an unquestioned source of truth. Review generated text, translations and factual claims for accuracy and local context before using or publishing them.

  1. Choose one low-risk task from study, teaching, work or a community project.
  2. Write a prompt explaining the task, audience, context and desired format.
  3. Check important claims against reliable sources and edit the result yourself.
  4. Record the task, tools and human checks in a small learning portfolio.

How to choose genuine free AI training

  • Confirm that the learning materials are free, not just registration.
  • Check whether certificates, graded assessments or exams have extra charges.
  • Review the provider, syllabus, course language and expected study time.
  • Confirm registration and access from Lithuania before beginning.
  • Never share passwords or confidential personal information with unverified sites or AI tools.
