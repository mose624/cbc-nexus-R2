Chapter overview and learning outcomes

This chapter develops a structured understanding of human physiology and homeostasis. Learners should connect core concepts to other biomedical disciplines, explain relationships rather than rely only on memorised lists, communicate scientific ideas accurately and recognise the limits of simplified models. Clinical examples are for education and discussion; patient assessment and care require qualified supervision and current local protocols.

  • Define and use the chapter's core terminology accurately.
  • Explain key structures, mechanisms, relationships and regulatory processes.
  • Integrate knowledge across relevant biomedical disciplines.
  • Interpret educational examples while acknowledging uncertainty and variation.
  • Apply patient-safety, ethical and professional principles to learning scenarios.
