CBE NEXUS EDUCATION GUIDE · TEACHING TIPS

How Can I Improve My English?

Build English through daily listening, speaking, reading and writing. Choose material at your level, learn useful phrases in context and practise consistently. Record yourself speaking, read short articles, keep a vocabulary notebook and write a short paragraph each day. Review errors and ask a teacher or language partner for feedback. The British Council LearnEnglish site offers guided practice for different skills and levels.

Practical steps and further guidance

Set a realistic 30-minute routine: listen for ten minutes, read for ten minutes and speak or write for ten minutes. Focus on communication first, then improve grammar and pronunciation. Track progress with practical goals such as holding a five-minute conversation or writing a clear email.
