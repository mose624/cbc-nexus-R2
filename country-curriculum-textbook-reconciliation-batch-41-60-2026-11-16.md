Scope: entries 41–60 in `country-curriculum-chapter-completion-register.js`, canonical alphabetical order.

## Audit rules

- A ministry portal, framework or examination syllabus is not automatically a complete grade-by-grade sequence.
- Distinguish national curriculum, local implementation, subject streams and examination pathways.
- A catalogue, syllabus or curriculum programme is not a textbook contents page. Do not assign textbook chapter counts unless the actual adopted edition's contents pages have been inspected.
- The status below is a source-reconciliation pass, not a claim that all grade topics have been transcribed.
