Which Countries Recruit Foreign Teachers?

Explore destinations that employ international teachers and the checks to make before applying.

Many countries employ teachers from overseas, particularly for international schools, language teaching, specialist subjects and hard-to-fill posts. Recruitment changes with school demand, visa rules and local teacher supply, so a country should never be treated as having guaranteed vacancies.

Destinations to research

  • United Arab Emirates: international and private schools recruit overseas teachers for various curricula. Check school and emirate requirements.
  • United Kingdom: eligible schools may sponsor qualified teachers for certain roles. Check current visa and qualification rules.
  • United States: international schools and eligible exchange programmes recruit teachers; visa routes and state requirements differ.
  • Canada: provincial teacher certification is important and requirements vary by province or territory.
  • Australia: teacher registration is handled by state and territory authorities, with overseas qualification assessment requirements.
  • Qatar, Saudi Arabia and other Gulf states: private and international schools recruit foreign teachers subject to employer and government requirements.

What employers commonly request

Expect to provide a recognised teaching qualification, relevant degree, experience, references, identity and background documents. Some roles require a teaching licence, curriculum experience or language proficiency.

How to compare destinations

Compare licensing, visa eligibility, salary after tax, housing, healthcare, contract length, school accreditation and cost of living. Confirm every detail with the employer and official government sources.
