Chapter 2: Fractions, ratio, proportion and percentages

A fraction represents part of a whole or a quotient. To add or subtract fractions, use a common denominator. To multiply fractions, multiply numerators and denominators; to divide, multiply by the reciprocal.

Core explanation

A ratio compares quantities in the same units. Simplify by dividing each part by the highest common factor. Direct proportion has the form y = kx; inverse proportion has the form y = k/x. Percentages are fractions out of 100.

Worked example / application

Worked example: share £84 in the ratio 3:4. There are 7 parts, so one part is £12; the shares are £36 and £48. A 15% increase multiplies by 1.15; a 15% decrease multiplies by 0.85.

Key learning point

Further textbook notes: ratio, proportion and percentages

Simplify a ratio by dividing every part by the same common factor. To share an amount in a ratio, add the parts, divide the total by this sum, then multiply by each part. Direct proportion has form y=kx; inverse proportion has form y=k/x. A percentage increase multiplies by 1+r/100; a decrease multiplies by 1−r/100. Repeated percentage change uses a multiplier repeatedly.

Worked example: Share 840 in the ratio 3:4. There are 7 parts, each worth 120, so the shares are 360 and 480.

Practise: Solve a reverse percentage problem and explain why a 20% increase followed by a 20% decrease does not return to the starting value.

Three additional worked examples — different approaches

Study each method, compare the reasoning and use the final check to verify your answer. These are original teaching examples designed to build flexible problem-solving skills.

Worked example 1: Method 1 — common denominator

Question: Evaluate 5/6 − 3/8.

Step-by-step solution: The lowest common denominator is 24. Convert: 5/6 = 20/24 and 3/8 = 9/24. Subtract: 20/24 − 9/24 = 11/24.

Check / exam insight: Keep the denominator unchanged after converting both fractions.

Worked example 2: Method 2 — unitary ratio method

Question: A recipe for 4 people uses 300 g of flour. How much is needed for 7 people?

Step-by-step solution: First find the amount for one person: 300 ÷ 4 = 75 g. Multiply by 7: 75 × 7 = 525 g.

Check / exam insight: The recipe scales directly with the number of people.

Worked example 3: Method 3 — reverse percentage multiplier

Question: After a 15% increase, a price is KSh 920. Find the original price.

Step-by-step solution: A 15% increase gives a multiplier of 1.15. Original price = 920 ÷ 1.15 = KSh 800.

Check / exam insight: Do not subtract 15% of 920; reverse a percentage change by dividing by its multiplier.

Worked question 1: Simplify 42/56.

Method and working: Divide numerator and denominator by their HCF, 14.

Final answer: 3/4

Worked question 2: Evaluate 2/3 × 9/10.

Method and working: Multiply numerators and denominators: 18/30, then simplify.

Final answer: 3/5

Worked question 3: Evaluate 5/6 ÷ 10/9.

Method and working: Multiply by the reciprocal: 5/6 × 9/10 = 45/60.

Final answer: 3/4

Worked question 4: Share 960 in the ratio 5:7.

Method and working: Total parts = 12. One part = 960/12 = 80. Shares = 400 and 560.

Final answer: 400 and 560

Worked question 5: Simplify the ratio 1.5:2.5.

Method and working: Multiply both parts by 2 to get 3:5.

Final answer: 3:5

Worked question 6: If 8 pens cost KSh 240, find the cost of 13 pens at the same rate.

Method and working: One pen costs 240/8 = 30. Cost for 13 = 13 × 30.

Final answer: KSh 390

Worked question 7: Find 35% of 640.

Method and working: 0.35 × 640 = 224.

Final answer: 224

Worked question 8: Increase 750 by 12%.

Method and working: Multiplier = 1.12. Calculate 750 × 1.12.

Final answer: 840

Worked question 9: Decrease 1,200 by 15%.

Method and working: Multiplier = 0.85. Calculate 1200 × 0.85.

Final answer: 1,020

Worked question 10: A sale price after 25% discount is KSh 900. Find the original price.

Method and working: The sale price is 75% of original. Original = 900/0.75.

Final answer: KSh 1,200

Worked question 11: A value changes from 50 to 65. Find the percentage change.

Method and working: Change = 15. Percentage change = 15/50 × 100.

Final answer: 30% increase

Worked question 12: Divide 84 in the ratio 2:3:7.

Method and working: Total parts = 12; one part = 7. Shares = 14, 21 and 49.

Final answer: 14, 21, 49

Worked question 13: If y is directly proportional to x and y=18 when x=6, find y when x=11.

Method and working: y = kx; k = 18/6 = 3. Then y = 3 × 11.

Final answer: 33

Worked question 14: If y is inversely proportional to x and y=8 when x=3, find y when x=12.

Method and working: xy = k = 24. Therefore y = 24/12.

Final answer: 2

Worked question 15: A quantity is shared in ratio 4:5. The smaller share is 28. Find the total.

Method and working: Four parts = 28, so one part = 7. Total nine parts = 63.

Final answer: 63

Worked question 16: A price of KSh 2,000 is increased by 10%, then reduced by 10%.

Method and working: 2000 × 1.10 × 0.90 = 1980.

Final answer: KSh 1,980; net decrease 1%

Worked question 17: A mixture uses juice and water in ratio 3:5. For 2 litres of juice, find water needed.

Method and working: Three parts = 2 L; one part = 2/3 L. Water is five parts = 10/3 L.

Final answer: 3⅓ litres

Worked example, exam-style question and marking guidance

Worked example: Share 720 in ratio 2:3:4. Total parts=9; one part=80; shares are 160, 240 and 320.
Exam-style question (3 marks)

A jacket costs 240 after a 20% discount. Find its original price.

Show model answer and mark allocation

Multiplier after discount=0.80 (1); original=240/0.80 (1); original price=300 (1).

Examiner’s explanation: Reverse percentages require division by the multiplier, not adding 20% of the discounted price.

Exam technique: Show each formula, substitution, calculation and unit. For “explain” questions, connect the scientific idea to the outcome; for “describe” questions, give the relevant sequence or features.

Extra chapter practice question (3 marks)

A quantity of 84 is shared in the ratio 3:4. Find the two shares.

Show answer and mark scheme

Total parts = 3 + 4 = 7 (1); one part = 84/7 = 12 (1); shares = 36 and 48 (1).
