Chapter overview and learning outcomes

This chapter develops an integrated understanding of internal medicine i: cardiovascular, respiratory and renal medicine. Learners should explain concepts accurately, connect mechanisms to other medical disciplines, interpret evidence critically and recognise the limits of simplified models. Patient-related activities require appropriate consent, qualified supervision and adherence to institutional and local standards.

  • Define principal terms and classifications.
  • Explain important structures, mechanisms and relationships.
  • Integrate knowledge across biomedical and clinical disciplines.
  • Analyse educational cases while acknowledging uncertainty.
  • Apply professional, ethical and patient-safety principles.
