How Do I Prepare for National Examinations?

Begin by mapping the official syllabus and examination format. Mark each topic as confident, developing or needing support. Create a weekly timetable with short study blocks, breaks and time for sleep. Use active recall: close your notes and explain a concept or answer questions from memory.

Practise and review

Practise legitimate past papers under timed conditions, show your working and review every error. Ask teachers for help with repeated difficulties and check the official examination body's current instructions. Avoid relying on rumours or leaked papers. Regular revision is more effective than last-minute cramming.
