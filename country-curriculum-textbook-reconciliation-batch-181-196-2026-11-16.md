## Evidence quality and important jurisdiction distinctions

- **Detailed official curriculum/textbook source portals found:** Tuvalu, Uganda, South Sudan is not part of this batch; Uganda's NCDC catalogue and curriculum structure are the strongest direct leads here.
- **Major official frameworks or subject-curriculum leads identified:** United Kingdom (four devolved systems), United States (state/local), UAE, Uruguay, Uzbekistan, Vietnam and Zambia.
- **Official source leads but detailed current subject documents still required:** Ukraine, Vanuatu, Venezuela, Yemen, Zimbabwe and Kosovo.
- **Vatican City:** no unified national curriculum sequence verified; school/provider-level investigation is required.
- **Actual textbook contents pages inspected in this pass:** 0.
- **Complete grade-by-grade topic sequences transcribed into the canonical register in this pass:** 0.

## Priority follow-up work

1. Uganda: download current NCDC lower-secondary syllabi and 2025 A-level syllabi for Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics; inspect textbook contents pages separately.
2. Tuvalu: retrieve the framework and subject syllabi, then identify actual approved Year 1–13 learning materials.
3. United Kingdom: create separate England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland mappings; add examination boards as separate assessment layers.
4. United States: choose state-by-state coverage or explicitly label any state sample; do not create a fictitious national sequence.
5. UAE, Vietnam, Uzbekistan and Uruguay: obtain current official subject annexes and map curriculum transition/implementation years.
6. Zambia, Kosovo and Ukraine: retrieve current official grade-level subject programmes and identify integrated-to-disciplinary science transitions.
7. Venezuela, Yemen, Vanuatu and Zimbabwe: verify the current authority, version, language and grade-level documents before mapping topics.
8. Vatican City: establish whether a national school curriculum exists for the intended learner population; otherwise record the relevant school/provider pathway and do not create an unsupported country-wide syllabus.
