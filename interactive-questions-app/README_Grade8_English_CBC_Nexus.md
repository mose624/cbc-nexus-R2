# Grade 8 English assessment added to CBC Nexus

The Grade 8 English subject now includes the supplied 30-question practice examination as a dedicated multiple-choice bank. It covers reading comprehension, critical thinking, grammar and language use, paragraph organisation, poetry, and cross-text analysis.

- Questions: 30 multiple-choice items, 1 mark each (30 marks total).
- Timing: CBC Nexus uses 2 minutes per question, for a 60-minute session.
- Answer key: suggested key and short explanations derived from the supplied questions; the paper did not provide a completed key.
- Status: original practice material, not an official KNEC examination. Check alignment against the current KICD Grade 8 English curriculum design before formal assessment.
- Existing website functionality, other grades and subject banks are retained.
- Online reporting, server-enforced single attempts, AI written marking and cross-device progress remain dependent on configuring and deploying the Supabase backend.
