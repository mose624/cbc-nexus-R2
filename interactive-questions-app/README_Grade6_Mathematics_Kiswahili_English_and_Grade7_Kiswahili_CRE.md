# CBC Nexus update — Grade 6 and Grade 7 assessments

This update adds or preserves the following Grade 6/7 assessment banks:

- Grade 6 Mathematics — 30 MCQs, 30 marks, 60 minutes at 2 minutes per question.
- Kiswahili Darasa la 6 — 30 MCQs, 30 marks, 60 minutes at 2 minutes per question.
- Grade 6 English Language — 30 MCQs, 30 marks, 60 minutes at 2 minutes per question.
- Kiswahili Darasa la 7 — 30 MCQs, 30 marks, with the original supplied comprehension passage represented in questions 1–5.
- Grade 7 Christian Religious Education (CRE) — 30 MCQs, 30 marks.

All Grade 6 assessment questions use derived suggested answer keys and explanatory feedback. The Grade 6 Mathematics question about Tuesday sales has been clarified to compare Tuesday with the average of Monday and Wednesday; the correct result is 5.5 loaves.

The existing CBC Nexus timer runs at two minutes per question. The Grade 6 papers supplied specify 60 minutes, so the timer is aligned. Some Grade 7 source papers specify 45 minutes, but the existing site timer remains two minutes per question unless configured otherwise.

These are practice assessments, not official KNEC examination papers. Review answer keys and curriculum coverage before formal use. Online storage, cross-device progress, server-enforced attempts, and AI marking still require the configured Supabase deployment and secure API setup described in the project documentation.
