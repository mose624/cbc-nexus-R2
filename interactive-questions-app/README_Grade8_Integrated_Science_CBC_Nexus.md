# CBC Nexus — Grade 8 Integrated Science addition

The full site now includes the supplied Grade 8 Integrated Science 30-MCQ practice paper. Existing subjects and learner workflow are retained.

- 30 questions, 30 marks.
- Suggested source time: 45 minutes; CBC Nexus setting: 2 minutes per question, or 60 minutes.
- The source included no answer-letter key, so suggested answers and concise reasoning were derived from the question text.
- Local schematic diagrams are included for atomic structure, metals, water hardness, photosynthesis, pollination, food chains, wetlands, curved mirrors and waves.
- Original practice material, not an official KNEC examination; verify against the applicable KICD design before formal use.

Open `cbenexus-all-grades/index.html` in a modern browser. The standalone file is `CBC_Nexus_Grade8_Integrated_Science_Added.html`. Online reporting and server-side attempt locking require Supabase configuration and deployment.
