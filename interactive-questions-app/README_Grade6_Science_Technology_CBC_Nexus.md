# Grade 6 Science & Technology — CBC Nexus

Added a 30-question multiple-choice HOTS practice assessment to the existing CBC Nexus project. The paper covers living things and the environment, human health, materials, forces, energy and electricity, Earth and space, conservation, scientific investigations and use of technology.

- 30 questions; 30 marks.
- Suggested answer key and explanations are included in the bank.
- The Grade 6 assessment hub includes a Science & Technology quick-access card.
- The site retains its current timer setup (2 minutes per question), which gives this paper 60 minutes as provided in the source.
- This is practice material, not an official KNEC examination. Check coverage against the current KICD Grade 6 curriculum design before formal use.

The paper is integrated in `index.html`. Online persistence, server-enforced attempt limits and AI marking require configuring and deploying the included Supabase backend; they are not activated solely by adding this paper.
