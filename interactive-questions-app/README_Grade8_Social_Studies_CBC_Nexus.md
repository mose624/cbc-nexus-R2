# CBC Nexus — Grade 8 Social Studies added

This build adds a dedicated Grade 8 Social Studies practice assessment to CBC Nexus.

## Assessment details
- 30 multiple-choice questions; 30 marks.
- Topics include map scale, soil and flood risk, settlement, cultural preservation, oral history, resources, governance, population, regional cooperation, environmental management and sustainable development.
- A suggested answer key and feedback explanations are included.
- The site-wide timer is 2 minutes per question, so this paper receives 60 minutes.
- Learner details, one-attempt flow and progress tracking remain part of the website.

## Important
The source paper did not include answer letters, so the suggested key was derived from the question wording and checked during integration. This is original practice material, not an official KNEC examination. The website remains a local package until hosted online. Server-backed reporting and server-enforced attempt restrictions require Supabase configuration and deployment.
