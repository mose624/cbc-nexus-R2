# Grade 8 CRE assessment — CBC Nexus

Added 30 multiple-choice HOTS practice questions to the Grade 8 `CRE` bank. The questions cover Bible teachings, the life and ministry of Jesus Christ, Christian moral values, the church and community, and applying biblical teachings to daily life. Each item is worth 1 mark and includes a suggested answer and explanatory feedback.

The provided paper stated 45 minutes. The shared CBC Nexus platform applies its existing two-minute-per-question timer, so this 30-item bank receives 60 minutes. This is original practice material and is not an official KNEC examination. Because the pasted paper did not provide the answer letters, the suggested answer key was derived from the question wording and should be reviewed by a teacher before formal assessment.
