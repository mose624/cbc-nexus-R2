Five fully worked IB-style problems — Chapter 1

Use these model solutions to practise clear mathematical communication. Show equivalent valid methods in your own working.

Worked problem 1. A water tank holds 2.4×10³ L. It is drained at 18 L/min. How long until 1.05×10³ L remains?
Solution and reasoning: Volume removed = 2400−1050=1350 L. Time = 1350/18 = 75 min.
Worked problem 2. A medicine concentration falls from 80 mg/L to 10 mg/L by repeated halving. How many half-lives have elapsed?
Solution and reasoning: 80→40→20→10: three halvings. If the half-life is 6 h, elapsed time = 3×6=18 h.
Worked problem 3. A length is measured as 12.6 cm to the nearest 0.1 cm. State the lower and upper bounds and the maximum absolute error.
Solution and reasoning: 12.55≤L<12.65 cm. Maximum absolute error = 0.05 cm.
Worked problem 4. Simplify (3.2×10⁵)(4×10⁻³) in standard form.
Solution and reasoning: 3.2×4×10^(5−3)=12.8×10²=1.28×10³.
Worked problem 5. An account of $750 earns 4.8% compound interest annually for 3 years. Find the final balance.
Solution and reasoning: A=750(1.048)³=$863.30 (to the nearest cent).

Five fully worked IB-style problems — Chapter 2

Use these model solutions to practise clear mathematical communication. Show equivalent valid methods in your own working.

Worked problem 1. An arithmetic sequence has u₃=11 and u₉=35. Find u₁, d and S₂₀.
Solution and reasoning: u₉−u₃=6d=24, so d=4. u₁=11−2(4)=3. S₂₀=10[2(3)+19(4)]=820.
Worked problem 2. A geometric sequence has u₂=12 and u₅=324, with positive common ratio. Find r and u₁.
Solution and reasoning: u₅/u₂=r³=324/12=27, so r=3. u₁=12/3=4.
Worked problem 3. Find the sum to infinity of 18−12+8−…
Solution and reasoning: a=18, r=−2/3. Since |r|<1, S∞=a/(1−r)=18/(5/3)=54/5=10.8.
Worked problem 4. The 4th term of an arithmetic sequence is 17 and the 12th is 49. Find the 30th term.
Solution and reasoning: 8d=32, d=4. u₁=17−3(4)=5. u₃₀=5+29(4)=121.
Worked problem 5. A ball rebounds to 70% of its previous height after each bounce. It is dropped from 2.5 m. Find the total vertical distance travelled before coming to rest.
Solution and reasoning: First drop=2.5 m. Rebound heights form 1.75+1.225+… with a=1.75, r=.7. Total=2.5+2(1.75/(1−.7))=14.166… m ≈14.2 m.

Five fully worked IB-style problems — Chapter 3

Use these model solutions to practise clear mathematical communication. Show equivalent valid methods in your own working.

Worked problem 1. Factorise 2x²−7x+3 and hence solve 2x²−7x+3=0.
Solution and reasoning: 2x²−7x+3=(2x−1)(x−3). Thus x=1/2 or x=3.
Worked problem 2. Simplify (x²−9)/(x²+5x+6), stating excluded values.
Solution and reasoning: (x−3)(x+3)/[(x+2)(x+3)]=(x−3)/(x+2), with x≠−2,−3.
Worked problem 3. Given p(x)=x³−4x²−x+4, show that x=1 is a root and factorise fully.
Solution and reasoning: p(1)=1−4−1+4=0. Divide by (x−1): x²−3x−4=(x−4)(x+1). Hence p(x)=(x−1)(x−4)(x+1).
Worked problem 4. Solve 3/(x−1)−2/(x+1)=1, stating restrictions.
Solution and reasoning: x≠±1. Multiply by x²−1: 3(x+1)−2(x−1)=x²−1. Thus x²−x−4=0, x=(1±√17)/2; neither is excluded.
Worked problem 5. Show that (a+b)²−(a−b)²=4ab, then evaluate it for a=7/2 and b=3.
Solution and reasoning: Expand: a²+2ab+b²−(a²−2ab+b²)=4ab. Substitution gives 4×(7/2)×3=42.

Worked problem 2. Solve simultaneously 2x+3y=13 and 3x−y=3.
Solution and reasoning: From 3x−y=3, y=3x−3. Substitute: 2x+9x−9=13, so x=2 and y=3.
Worked problem 3. A rectangle has perimeter 34 cm and area 60 cm². Find its side lengths.
Solution and reasoning: Let sides be x,y. x+y=17 and xy=60. Roots of t²−17t+60=0 are 5 and 12. Sides: 5 cm and 12 cm.

Solution and reasoning: 2x−5=7 gives x=6; 2x−5=−7 gives x=−1.

Solution and reasoning: 2x+3(x+8)=76 ⇒5x=52 ⇒x=10.40. Pass=$18.40.

Five fully worked IB-style problems — Chapter 5

Use these model solutions to practise clear mathematical communication. Show equivalent valid methods in your own working.

Worked problem 1. For f(x)=x²−6x+5, find the vertex, roots and range.
Solution and reasoning: f=(x−3)²−4. Vertex (3,−4). Roots from (x−1)(x−5)=0: x=1,5. Range f(x)≥−4.
Worked problem 2. The line through (−2,7) and (4,−5) is written y=mx+c. Find m,c and its x-intercept.
Solution and reasoning: m=(−5−7)/(4+2)=−2. Using (−2,7): c=3. Set y=0: x=3/2.
Worked problem 3. A function is f(x)=2(x−1)²−8. State the transformations of y=x² and find its zeros.
Solution and reasoning: Translate right 1, stretch vertically by factor 2, then down 8. Zeros: 2(x−1)²=8 ⇒(x−1)²=4 ⇒x=−1,3.
Worked problem 4. A model h(t)=−5t²+20t+1 gives height in metres. Find the time and height at the maximum.
Solution and reasoning: Vertex time t=−20/[2(−5)]=2 s. h(2)=−20+40+1=21 m.
Worked problem 5. Given f(x)=3x−2 and g(x)=x²+1, find f∘g and g∘f, then evaluate each at x=2.
Solution and reasoning: f(g(x))=3(x²+1)−2=3x²+1; at 2 it is 13. g(f(x))=(3x−2)²+1; at 2 it is 17.

Five fully worked IB-style problems — Chapter 6

Use these model solutions to practise clear mathematical communication. Show equivalent valid methods in your own working.

Worked problem 1. A population follows P(t)=500(1.06)^t. Find P(8), to the nearest whole organism.
Solution and reasoning: P(8)=500(1.06)^8≈796 organisms.
Worked problem 2. Solve 3^(2x−1)=81.
Solution and reasoning: 81=3⁴, so 2x−1=4 and x=2.5.
Worked problem 3. A radioactive sample has half-life 12 years. What fraction remains after 45 years?
Solution and reasoning: Fraction=(1/2)^(45/12)≈0.0743, so about 7.43% remains.
Worked problem 4. Solve log₂(x−1)+log₂(x+1)=3, stating the domain.
Solution and reasoning: Domain x>1. Combine: log₂(x²−1)=3 ⇒x²−1=8 ⇒x=3 (reject −3).
Worked problem 5. An investment grows from $1200 to $1500 in 5 years with annual compound growth. Find the annual percentage rate.
Solution and reasoning: 1500=1200(1+r)^5 ⇒r=(1.25)^(1/5)−1≈0.04564, or 4.56% p.a.
