# Evidence extraction: Afghanistan — Grade 7
Date: 16 November 2026  
Country register position: 1 of 196  
Authority: Afghanistan Ministry of Education (MoE)  
Evidence scope: official Ministry-hosted Grade 7 student textbooks; actual PDF contents pages inspected. This is a textbook contents extraction, **not yet an independent syllabus-PDF extraction**.

## Important version warning
The Ministry's public curriculum-books catalogue lists Mathematics and, for secondary grades, Biology, Chemistry and Physics. The Ministry also published a 2026 notice about reviewing and strengthening draft Science and Mathematics textbook content for Grades 7–9. Consequently, the PDFs below are verified Ministry-hosted historical editions, but their status as the currently approved/revised 2026 editions remains unresolved. Do not silently represent them as confirmed current editions.

## Transcribed textbook contents — Grade 7

### Mathematics — 8 chapters
1. Sets — p. 3
2. Natural numbers — p. 27
3. Integers (positive and negative numbers) — p. 71
4. Rational numbers — p. 97
5. Triangles and polygons — p. 119
6. Parallel and perpendicular lines — p. 157
7. Statistics and data handling — p. 187
8. Probability — p. 211

Source: Ministry PDF, book metadata and contents pages, pp. preliminary/contents; online PDF lines 39–50 and 68–147. The contents list includes subtopics such as set operations, divisibility and factorisation, powers, HCF/LCM, square/cube roots, integer operations, rational operations, triangle angle/side properties, quadrilaterals, data collection, samples, frequency tables, mode/mean and probability experiments.

### Biology — 8 chapters
1. Cell: structure and function — p. 1
2. Cellular organization: tissues, organs, systems and organisms — p. 11
3. Cell processes: transport, diffusion, osmosis, photosynthesis and cellular respiration — p. 19
4. Cell division: amitosis, mitosis and meiosis — p. 29
5. Seed-bearing plants: types, roots, stems, growth and leaves — p. 37
6. Reproduction in seed-bearing plants: flowers, fruits, pollination and asexual reproduction — p. 49
7. Ecology: ecosystems and biotic/abiotic factors — p. 63
8. Cycles in ecosystems: energy transfer and water/carbon cycles — p. 71

Source: Ministry PDF, contents pages. Detailed contents entries include microscope and cell organelles; plant and animal tissues; diffusion and osmosis; photosynthesis and respiration; mitosis/meiosis; plant organs and leaf structure; flower structure, pollination and fruit; ecosystem factors and interactions; energy transfer and water/carbon cycles.

### Chemistry — 4 chapters
1. Matter and its properties — p. 1
2. Types of matter: mixtures, conservation of mass in solutions, solubility and pure substances — p. 21
3. Chemical reactions and equations: reactions, compound formation and balancing equations — p. 37
4. Important elements in life: hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen and carbon — p. 57

Source: Ministry PDF, contents page and introductory chapter description. The contents also list particles (atoms and molecules), states of matter, heat effects, mixtures, solubility and chemical equations.

### Physics — 7 chapters
1. Measurement: quantities, instruments, length, time, mass and accuracy — p. 1
2. Force: effects, units, measurement and vector properties — p. 17
3. Work, energy and power — p. 26
4. Pressure: atmospheric and liquid pressure, Pascal's law and Archimedes' principle — p. 40
5. Properties of light: vision, rectilinear propagation, shadows and transparent materials — p. 53
6. Reflection of light: laws, plane and spherical mirrors — p. 63
7. Refraction of light: prisms, lenses, eye and microscope — p. 77

Source: Ministry PDF, contents pages. The book introduction explicitly describes seven chapters and emphasizes observation, investigation, experiments and activities. Note the conflicting publication-year metadata (cover 1398; book-details section 1399).
