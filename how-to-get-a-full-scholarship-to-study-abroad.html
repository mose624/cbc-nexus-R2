How Can I Get a Full Scholarship to Study Abroad?

A practical step-by-step guide to finding, preparing for and applying for fully funded international scholarships.

A full scholarship can cover some or all of the major costs of studying abroad, such as tuition, accommodation, travel, health insurance and living expenses. The benefits differ by programme, so always read the official award conditions.

1. Choose a course and destination

Identify the subject and qualification you want, then shortlist countries and universities that offer suitable programmes. Check entry requirements, language requirements and whether the course is open to international applicants.

2. Search official scholarship programmes

Look at government-funded and university-funded opportunities. Examples include Chevening Scholarships (UK), DAAD scholarships (Germany), Fulbright programmes (United States) and Erasmus+ opportunities. Availability and eligibility vary by country and year.

3. Prepare a strong application

Common documents include academic transcripts, certificates, a passport, a CV, recommendation letters, a statement of purpose and proof of language ability. Some awards require work experience, leadership evidence, a research proposal or an admission offer.

4. Meet the deadline and submit safely

Use the scholarship provider's official website to confirm dates, required documents and submission instructions. Never pay an individual who promises to guarantee a scholarship. Keep copies of everything you submit.

5. Track your application

Record the deadline, reference number, interview date and next steps. Apply to several suitable programmes rather than relying on one award.

Remember: “Fully funded” does not mean every scholarship covers the same expenses. Confirm exactly what is included before accepting an offer.
