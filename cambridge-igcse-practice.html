Biology — Original Practice

1. Cell structure [4 marks]

(a) Name the cell structure that controls the activities of a cell. (1)
(b) State two structures found in a plant cell but not in a typical animal cell. (2)
(c) Explain the function of the cell membrane. (1)

Show answer and marking guidance

(a) Nucleus. (1)
(b) Any two: cell wall, chloroplast, large permanent vacuole. (1 each)
(c) Controls movement of substances into and out of the cell; it is selectively permeable. (1)

2. Enzymes [5 marks]

A student investigates the effect of temperature on an enzyme. Explain why the rate of enzyme activity increases as temperature rises towards the optimum, and why it decreases rapidly above the optimum.

Show answer and marking guidance

As temperature rises, particles gain kinetic energy and collide more frequently, so more enzyme-substrate complexes form. Above the optimum, bonds maintaining the enzyme's shape are disrupted; the active site changes shape (denaturation), so the substrate is less complementary and fewer complexes form. Award up to 5 marks for these linked points.

3. Photosynthesis [4 marks]

(a) Write the word equation for photosynthesis. (2)
(b) State two factors that can limit the rate of photosynthesis. (2)

Show answer and marking guidance

(a) Carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen, in the presence of light and chlorophyll. (2)
(b) Any two: light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration, temperature, chlorophyll availability. (1 each)

Chemistry — Original Practice

1. Atomic structure [4 marks]

An atom has proton number 17 and nucleon number 35. Determine its number of (a) protons, (b) neutrons and (c) electrons, assuming the atom is neutral.

Show answer and marking guidance

(a) 17 protons. (1)
(b) 35 − 17 = 18 neutrons. (1)
(c) 17 electrons because a neutral atom has equal numbers of protons and electrons. (2)

2. Bonding [4 marks]

Explain why sodium chloride has a high melting point and why it conducts electricity when molten but not when solid.

Show answer and marking guidance

It has a giant ionic lattice (1) with strong electrostatic attractions between oppositely charged ions (1), requiring much energy to overcome. When molten, ions are mobile and carry charge (1); in the solid lattice, ions are fixed in position and cannot move (1).

3. Acids and bases [4 marks]

Hydrochloric acid reacts with sodium hydroxide. (a) Name the type of reaction. (1) (b) Write the balanced symbol equation. (2) (c) Name the salt formed. (1)

Show answer and marking guidance

(a) Neutralisation. (1)
(b) HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O. (2)
(c) Sodium chloride. (1)

Physics — Original Practice

1. Speed and acceleration [4 marks]

A car increases its velocity uniformly from 5 m/s to 25 m/s in 4 seconds. Calculate its acceleration and state the equation used.

Show answer and marking guidance

Acceleration = change in velocity ÷ time (1). a = (25 − 5) ÷ 4 (1) = 5 m/s² (2).

2. Electrical circuits [4 marks]

A 6 Ω resistor is connected to a 12 V supply. Calculate the current through the resistor and the electrical power dissipated.

Show answer and marking guidance

Using V = IR: I = 12 ÷ 6 = 2 A (2). Power P = VI = 12 × 2 = 24 W (2).

3. Density [3 marks]

A metal block has a mass of 540 g and a volume of 200 cm³. Calculate its density in g/cm³.

Show answer and marking guidance

Density = mass ÷ volume (1) = 540 ÷ 200 (1) = 2.7 g/cm³ (1).

Mathematics — Original Practice

1. Algebra [3 marks]

Solve the equation 3(2x − 1) = 4x + 9. Show your working.

Show answer and marking guidance

6x − 3 = 4x + 9 (1); 2x = 12 (1); x = 6 (1).

2. Percentage [3 marks]

A laptop costs KSh 48,000. Its price is reduced by 15%. Calculate the sale price.

Show answer and marking guidance

Discount = 0.15 × 48,000 = KSh 7,200 (2). Sale price = 48,000 − 7,200 = KSh 40,800 (1).

3. Pythagoras' theorem [3 marks]

A right-angled triangle has perpendicular sides of 6 cm and 8 cm. Calculate the length of the hypotenuse.

Show answer and marking guidance

c² = 6² + 8² (1) = 36 + 64 = 100 (1), so c = 10 cm (1).
