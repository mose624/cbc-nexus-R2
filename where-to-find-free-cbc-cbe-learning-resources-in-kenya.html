Where Can Teachers Find Free CBC and CBE Learning Resources in Kenya?

Learn how to find, check and organise free CBC and CBE notes, schemes of work, lesson plans and assessment materials in Kenya.

Teachers need reliable, well-organised materials that support curriculum delivery without replacing professional judgement. Free notes, schemes of work, lesson plans, assessment tasks and revision materials can save preparation time, but every resource should be checked for accuracy, relevance and alignment with the current curriculum.

1. Identify the resource you actually need

Start with the grade, learning area, strand, sub-strand and intended learning outcome. Decide whether you need learner notes, a lesson plan, a scheme of work, an assessment rubric, a project idea or revision questions. A specific search is more effective than downloading large collections without a plan.

2. Check curriculum alignment

Compare the resource with current curriculum designs and official guidance from the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD). Check terminology, scope, sequence, learner level and expected competencies. Flag outdated materials and do not label a resource KICD-approved unless that status is genuinely documented.

3. Review quality before classroom use

Check factual accuracy, spelling, accessibility, inclusivity and suitability for the learners. For science materials, verify explanations, units, diagrams and safety instructions. For assessments, make sure questions measure the intended learning outcome and that marking guidance is clear.

4. Adapt materials to your learners

A resource is a starting point, not a substitute for planning. Adjust examples, language, pacing and activities to local context, available materials and learner needs. Provide alternative ways for learners to participate where appropriate.

5. Organise your digital resource library

Use folders by grade, learning area, strand and resource type. Keep file names descriptive and record the source and date checked. This makes it easier to update materials when curriculum guidance changes.

6. Respect copyright and sharing conditions

Use resources according to their licence or permission. Do not remove authorship notices or redistribute paid materials without permission. When creating original materials, keep a record of sources used and clearly distinguish your own work from adapted content.

Frequently asked questions

Are all free downloads automatically curriculum-approved?

No. Free availability does not establish official approval. Check the source and compare the material with current KICD curriculum guidance.

What resources should a teacher prepare first?

Prioritise the scheme of work, lesson preparation, learning activities and assessment methods for the next teaching unit.

Where can I access CBE Nexus resources?

Visit the CBE Nexus resource library to browse available learning materials and check the details of each resource.
