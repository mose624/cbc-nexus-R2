## Evidence quality and chapter-entry gate

- **Official curriculum, syllabus or textbook-portal source leads located:** Madagascar, Malaysia, Maldives, Malta, Mauritius, Micronesia (FSM), Moldova, Morocco (reform status only), Mozambique.
- **Actual textbook contents pages inspected:** 0. Madagascar's surfaced PDF is a programme document with a table of contents; Mozambique and Mauritius provide textbook portals, but individual textbook contents pages were not opened and transcribed.
- **Complete official grade-by-grade topic sequences transcribed into the canonical register:** 0.
- **Targeted official-source gaps:** Malawi, Mali, Marshall Islands, Mauritania, Mexico, Monaco, Mongolia, Montenegro, Myanmar, Namibia and Nauru.
- **Current-status cautions:** Maldives' surfaced framework and FSM standards are older; Madagascar's surfaced detailed programme files date from 2015–2016; Morocco's July 2026 item describes a preliminary framework review, not a completed replacement curriculum.
