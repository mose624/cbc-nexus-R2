How to check that Cambridge notes match your syllabus

  1. Confirm the qualification and stage. Choose Primary, Lower Secondary, IGCSE or AS & A Level.
  2. Check the subject and syllabus code. Some subjects have separate syllabus variants or components.
  3. Confirm the examination session. Use the syllabus version that applies to your examination year.
  4. Open published chapters only. Review the chapter title and learning coverage; do not assume the full syllabus is complete.
  5. Cross-check official documents. Use Cambridge International's current syllabus and resource information for final requirements.

Editorial note: CBE Nexus notes are original supplementary learning materials. They are not official Cambridge publications, mark schemes or examination papers.

Frequently asked questions about Cambridge online notes

Are all Cambridge IGCSE subjects and chapters available in the notes reader?

No. The subject directory and published chapter collection are different. Only chapters already published in the reader are available; remaining topics are being developed and checked against the relevant syllabus.

Are CBE Nexus Cambridge notes official Cambridge materials?

No. They are original supplementary learning notes from CBE Nexus. Use Cambridge International's official website and resource centre for official syllabuses, examination information and materials released by Cambridge.

How do I choose the right IGCSE syllabus variant?

Check the subject name, syllabus code, component options and examination session with your school and the official Cambridge syllabus documents before using a revision plan.

Can I use the same notes for every Cambridge examination year?

Not automatically. Syllabus content and assessment arrangements can change. Confirm that the chapter and syllabus version match the examination session you are preparing for.
