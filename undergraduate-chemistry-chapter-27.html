Key equations and fully worked calculations

Beer–Lambert: A = εlc. Retention factor: k′ = (tR − tM)/tM. Resolution: Rs = 2(tR,2 − tR,1)/(w1 + w2), using baseline peak widths.

Calibration: response = slope × concentration + intercept; estimate unknown concentration only within the validated range.

Worked calculation A: UV-visible concentration

A solution has absorbance 0.600 at a wavelength where ε = 1.50 × 10⁴ L mol⁻¹ cm⁻¹. With a 1.00 cm cuvette, c = A/(εl) = 0.600/(1.50 × 10⁴ × 1.00) = 4.00 × 10⁻⁵ mol L⁻¹ = 40.0 μmol L⁻¹.

Worked calculation B: chromatographic retention

An analyte has retention time tR = 6.50 min and an unretained marker time tM = 1.00 min. k′ = (6.50 − 1.00)/1.00 = 5.50. The analyte is retained 5.5 times the dead-time interval under these conditions.

Worked calculation C: method precision

Replicate results are 9.8, 10.0, 10.1, 9.9 and 10.2 mg L⁻¹. The mean is 10.0 mg L⁻¹. Calculate the sample standard deviation using n−1 in the denominator, then report mean ± SD and the number of replicates; precision alone does not establish accuracy.
