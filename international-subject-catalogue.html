SUBJECT DIRECTORY & STUDY LIBRARY

All International Curriculum Subjects

Subject catalogue for Pearson Edexcel International GCSE and International Advanced Level, plus IB Diploma Programme and Middle Years Programme. Each curriculum remains separate, with its own levels, subject groups and specification requirements.

Coverage transparency: The directory lists the official subject range. The published CBE Nexus chapter-note libraries are being expanded in stages. A subject marked “Chapter notes published” has some online chapters available; “Notes in development” means its complete chapter-by-chapter notes have not yet been published. We do not label a syllabus complete until every current specification topic has been checked.
