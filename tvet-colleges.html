TVET Colleges in Kenya: applicant guide

Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) develops practical skills for employment, entrepreneurship and further learning. TVET institutions offer programmes across trades, technology, business, hospitality, agriculture, construction, applied sciences and other occupational areas. The right choice depends on the skill you want and the qualification level required.

Understand TVET qualification levels

TVET programmes may lead to artisan, craft, certificate and diploma qualifications, with progression routes depending on the course and entry requirements. Qualification frameworks and assessment arrangements differ. Check the exact award title, level, assessment body and progression opportunities.

Choose a course by occupation

Start with the job or business activity you want. Compare course units with the practical tasks used in that occupation. For technical trades, ask about workshops, tools, safety training and supervised practical assessment.

Check accreditation and assessment

Verify the institution and programme with the relevant TVET regulatory and quality-assurance authorities. Ask who assesses the course, how practical competence is demonstrated, and what certificate or diploma is awarded.

Industrial attachment and employment readiness

Many vocational pathways benefit from attachment, apprenticeship or workplace exposure. Ask whether the institution arranges placements, their duration and supervision, and whether learners must source an employer.

Admissions, fees and progression

Confirm entry requirements, dates, fees, tools or protective equipment costs, accommodation and assessment charges. Ask whether credits can transfer to a higher qualification and what conditions apply.

Frequently asked questions

Is TVET only for students who do not qualify for university?

No. TVET is a skills-focused pathway for learners with different backgrounds and career goals.

Can I progress from certificate to diploma?

Often there are progression routes, but eligibility depends on the programme and institution.

Should I check the assessment body?

Yes. Confirm the awarding or assessment body and exact qualification before enrolling.
