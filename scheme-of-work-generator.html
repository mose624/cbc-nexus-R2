1. Scheme details

Choose from the available learning areas. Subjects vary by grade; this menu provides the full current list of options in this generator.
This list shows only curriculum units currently available in the verified catalogue.
Used when building a full-term schedule.

Choose a grade, subject and verified curriculum unit, then select the strand, sub-strand and the learning outcomes range you want to scheme from and up to. The dropdown catalogue expands as each official KICD curriculum design is transcribed and verified; unavailable units are not filled with invented outcomes.

2. Choose topics and subtopics

Enter one topic or sub-topic per line, or paste a list from your approved syllabus / KICD curriculum design. This topic list is used to organise the lesson rows; it is not presented as verified KICD data unless it came from the selected digitised unit.

The generator will distribute the entered topics across your teaching weeks and lessons per week, while leaving outcomes, activities, inquiry questions, resources and assessment editable for your curriculum.

3. Preview and customise your scheme

Select a digitised KICD unit and load five lessons. Strand, sub-strand, specific learning outcomes, learning experiences, key inquiry questions, resources and assessment will be filled from the curriculum design data where available. All cells remain editable.

KICD source: Official Grade 10 curriculum designs. Only units listed in the selector have been transcribed so far; do not treat an unlisted subject as automatically verified.
