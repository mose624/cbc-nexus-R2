What Are the Best Free Online Courses With Certificates?

How to find credible free online courses, understand certificate fees and choose a course that supports your career.

Free online courses allow learners to develop skills without attending a physical classroom. Some courses provide free certificates, while others let you study for free but charge for a verified certificate. Check the terms before enrolling.

Where to look

  • OpenLearn offers free learning materials and statements of participation on eligible courses.
  • edX offers course access options; verified certificates may require payment.
  • Coursera offers courses from universities and organisations; financial aid or free access may be available on selected courses.
  • Alison provides free study access, with certificate and diploma conditions that should be checked on each course.
  • Khan Academy provides free lessons and practice, though it is not primarily a certificate platform.

How to choose a useful course

Start with a skill you need for work or study. Check the course provider, syllabus, instructor, duration, assessment method and whether the certificate is included. Compare the course outcomes with job or professional requirements.

How to complete the course

Set a weekly study timetable, complete the exercises, save your work and download your certificate or completion record where offered. Add relevant learning to your CV honestly, distinguishing a course-completion certificate from a professional licence or accredited qualification.
