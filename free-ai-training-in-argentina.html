How to choose a genuine free AI course

  • Confirm that the course content is free, not only the registration page.
  • Check whether graded assessments, badges or certificates have separate fees.
  • Review the provider, syllabus, update date and expected study time.
  • Check whether the course works in your country and preferred language.
  • Do not share passwords, payment details or confidential learner information with unverified providers or AI tools.
