How to Generate, Pay for and Download Your CV

  1. Complete your details and select the CV package and preferred design.
  2. Generate your professional CV and review the preview carefully. You can refine it before downloading.
  3. Choose Pay to Download CV. The applicable package fee is Basic KSh 200, Classic KSh 400 or Professional KSh 800.
  4. Enter your Safaricom M-Pesa number and approve the STK Push on your phone using your M-Pesa PIN.
  5. Save your CV as a PDF when payment is confirmed and the print window opens. Select “Save as PDF” in your browser's print options.

Important: Check your details and the CV preview before paying. Keep this page open while your payment is being verified. If you need help, use the WhatsApp support link above.
