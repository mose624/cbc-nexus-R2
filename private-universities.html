Private Universities in Kenya: applicant guide

Private universities are an important part of Kenya’s higher-education landscape. They offer different institutional missions, learning environments and programme portfolios. Applicants should compare recognition, programme status, fees and admissions directly before making a decision.

Understand the institution and its status

Check the institution’s current legal and accreditation status with the Commission for University Education (CUE). Distinguish a chartered university from an institution operating under another approved status, and check whether the specific programme is approved. Recognition of an institution should not be treated as automatic approval of every course.

Compare academic fit

Review the curriculum, specialisations, teaching staff, practical facilities and research opportunities. For health, education, law, engineering and other regulated fields, check additional professional-body requirements. Ask whether the course is offered at your chosen campus and study mode.

Understand fees and financial planning

Request the current official fee structure for your intake. Ask about application fees, tuition by semester, examination charges, accommodation, equipment, fieldwork, clinical placement and graduation costs. Confirm scholarship or instalment terms in writing.

Application documents and timelines

Requirements may include academic certificates, transcripts, identification and programme-specific evidence. International applicants may need certified translations, equivalence checks or immigration documentation. Use the official admissions checklist.

Verify before accepting an offer

Check that the offer comes from an official channel, and that programme and campus details match your application. Use institution-controlled payment accounts and retain receipts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a private university is recognised?

Check current CUE institutional status and confirm programme approval where applicable.

Do private universities use KUCCPS?

Some programmes and applicant categories may appear in KUCCPS processes; many universities also accept direct applications.

Can fees differ by campus or mode?

Yes. Confirm the fee schedule for the exact programme, campus, mode and intake.
