What Are the Best Universities in the World?

Use rankings to build a shortlist, but compare the actual subject and programme you want to study. Check course content, teaching approach, facilities, research opportunities, student support and professional accreditation where relevant. Overall rankings may not reflect the strength of a specific course.

What to verify before applying

Confirm that the institution and programme are recognised by the relevant authority. Compare tuition and living costs, scholarships, internships and graduate outcomes. For regulated careers, check whether the qualification meets the professional regulator's requirements in the country where you plan to work.
