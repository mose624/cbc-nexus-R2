Frequently asked questions about university courses and units

Where can I find university courses with units by year of study?

Use the course search above, open a programme and review its illustrative Year 1, Year 2 and later-year units. Each course also separates semester one and semester two subject areas.

Are these the official units offered by every university?

No. These are general study guides, not official handbooks. Universities, countries and accreditation bodies set their own course titles, credit values, prerequisites, semester calendars and placements. Confirm the current approved plan with the institution.

Which university courses are market-relevant?

Digital technology, data and AI, cybersecurity, healthcare, engineering, finance, education, supply chains, agriculture and sustainability are useful areas to investigate. Demand varies by country and changes over time; no course guarantees employment.

How do I check whether a university course is accredited?

Check the university's official programme page and the relevant national higher-education or professional regulator. For regulated careers such as medicine, nursing, pharmacy, law, engineering and teaching, verify registration and supervised-practice requirements before enrolling.

Can I use these course guides to choose a degree abroad?

Yes, as an initial comparison checklist. Compare entry requirements, tuition, language of instruction, duration, recognition in the country where you plan to work, practical placements and graduate outcomes using official sources.

Important programme and professional checks

  • Confirm the current course title, award, duration and units directly with the institution.
  • Check programme accreditation and recognition with the relevant national authority.
  • Medicine, nursing, law, engineering and teaching may require additional registration, supervised practice or professional examinations.
  • Do not use this general guide as a substitute for an official university handbook or regulator's requirements.
