Choose how you want to access resources

Explore available resource names, grades and subjects in the Resource Library before paying. Your payment must be independently verified before paid downloads are unlocked. Kenyan customers can use M-Pesa; international customers can use Paystack card checkout in USD.

Free resources stay free. KSh 0 resources remain downloadable without a subscription or package.

Access plans cover approved paid resources across the library for the selected time period. Grade packages cover approved paid resources for that one grade only. Grades 10–12 keep their existing individual-resource prices and checkout; these optional plans do not change those prices.
