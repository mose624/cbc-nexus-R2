What Qualifications Do I Need to Become a Teacher?

Requirements depend on country, school level and subject. Common pathways include an approved early-years or primary teacher qualification, a subject degree combined with teacher education for secondary teaching, or a relevant technical qualification with trainer preparation for vocational education.

Confirm local requirements

Employers and regulators may require supervised teaching practice, teacher registration, background checks, language proficiency and professional development. Identify the country and level first, then confirm exact requirements with its official teacher-registration authority. A qualification accepted in one country may need evaluation elsewhere.

International standards: expanded guidance

Choose the correct teacher-education route

Qualifications vary by early childhood, primary, secondary, special education and vocational teaching. Common routes include an approved teacher-education degree or a subject degree followed by recognised teacher preparation. Secondary teachers may need strong academic preparation in the subject they intend to teach. Check course approval with the relevant regulator before enrolling.

Complete supervised teaching practice

Teacher preparation normally includes practical classroom experience under supervision. Develop evidence of lesson planning, classroom management, assessment, inclusion, safeguarding and reflective practice. Keep placement records and mentor feedback if required for registration.

Meet local licensing and suitability rules

Some jurisdictions require registration before teaching in regulated schools. England uses Qualified Teacher Status routes; Ontario requires certification by the Ontario College of Teachers for publicly funded schools. Other countries, states and provinces have separate authorities. Police checks, professional standing, language proficiency and document verification may also be required.

Keep professional learning current

Teachers should update subject knowledge, pedagogy, assessment, inclusive practice, digital literacy and child safeguarding. Some regulators require continuing professional development or registration renewal. Keep a record of training, but do not confuse optional online course certificates with the formal qualification or licence needed to teach.

Check recognition before working abroad

Ask the destination regulator whether your qualification is comparable, whether additional study or supervised practice is needed, and how to submit transcripts or professional standing documents. Immigration permission is a separate process from teacher registration.
