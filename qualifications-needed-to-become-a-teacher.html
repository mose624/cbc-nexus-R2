What Qualifications Do I Need to Become a Teacher?

Requirements depend on country, school level and subject. Common pathways include an approved early-years or primary teacher qualification, a subject degree combined with teacher education for secondary teaching, or a relevant technical qualification with trainer preparation for vocational education.

Confirm local requirements

Employers and regulators may require supervised teaching practice, teacher registration, background checks, language proficiency and professional development. Identify the country and level first, then confirm exact requirements with its official teacher-registration authority. A qualification accepted in one country may need evaluation elsewhere.
